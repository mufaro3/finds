Differential Calculation Functions
==================================

These formulas are described in :cite:t:`mabrouk2024` and :cite:t:`mabrouk2025`. For this simulation, the system of fish are stored the following format:

.. math::

   \mathbf{X} := \begin{bmatrix}
   x_1 & y_1 & z_1 & n_{1x} & n_{1y} & n_{1z} \\
   x_2 & y_2 & z_2 & n_{2x} & n_{2y} & n_{2z} \\
   \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
   x_N & y_N & z_N & n_{Nx} & n_{Ny} & n_{Nz}
   \end{bmatrix}
