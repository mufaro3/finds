\renewcommand{\maketitlehookd}{
  \begin{abstract}
    \noindent
    It is well known that animals travelling in cohesive groups yields many
    collective and individual benefits, such as security against predators and
    energy efficiency. As such, the replication of such benefits for analogous,
    biology-inspired technologies like autonomous drone systems requires keen
    understanding of the cohesion and evolution of systems of these groups,
    but these investigations are fundamentally limited to small sizes due to
    the high computational complexity in computing pairwise dynamics at order
    $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$. As real schools of fish have been recorded to contain
    millions of members, FINDS, or Fish INteraction and Dynamics Simulator,
    was created to enable the efficient simulation of schools of fish by
    combining a simple, particle-like mathematical model of swimmers with
    cutting-edge algorithms and post-processing techniques utilized in particle
    dynamics and astrophysics simulation libraries such as GADGET-4 and
    REBOUND. FINDS is implemented as a containerized, modular software suite
    written predominantly in C (with Python for post-processing) to compute the
    time-derivative for each state of the system of fish using brute-force
    computation, Barnes-Hut approximaiton, or the Fast-Multipole Method,
    then numerically integrate this derivative using adaptive time-stepping,
    and then lastly, optionally process the calculated dataset to produce
    various analytic plots as needed.
  \end{abstract}
}

\section{Acknowledgements}

In the development of FINDS, Alvin Ng's \texttt{grav\_sim} was used extensively
as a reference for implementing a fast $N$-body simulator in C, particularly
in the implementation of the Barnes-Hut algorithm. In addition, the direct
advice of Alvin Ng was instrumental to the implementation of linear octrees
and parallelization.

Furthermore, generative artificial intelligence (OpenAI's ChatGPT-5.5 and
Google's Gemini 3.3) were used heavily for initial code generation before
being thoroughly tested and validated to ensure correctness. Artificial
intelligence was not used in developing the content of this report, including
the abstract, sections, and figures.
